            Controls & shortcuts
            Moving and looking

            Move with WASD at a walk, and hold Shift to run. Shallow water can be forded at a crawl; deep water cannot. The player always faces the pointer. Space centres the camera back on the player, M mutes the feedback sounds, and dragging with the middle button — or with Shift held and the left one — pans while the wheel zooms. , and . turn the view a twelfth of a circle at a time. The right button is never the camera: it is how you work a hex.

            Panels

            B opens the construction catalogue, I the cargo pack, O research, C creative mode, and P this panel. Esc closes whatever is open and returns to the inspect tool, which is the one panel that stays on the world.

            Creative mode

            C opens it, and the title screen can start a run in it. It researches everything, makes building and demolition free, gives you any material in the catalogue, and widens the pack to as many as 240 slots. Machines still run at their normal rates, so what works here works in a priced run too. Turning it off restores the prices and keeps the research.

            Field actions

            Hold F to keep gathering from the field you are standing on or beside, and X to hand the landing hub whatever it is asking for. To work one particular hex, hold the right button on it: holding keeps harvesting the cell you are pointing at, as far as your own reach goes, and dragging walks the harvest along to the next one. The ring that closes around you is the wait until the next action.

            The bar, and what goes on it

            B opens the catalogue: everything you can build, grouped by what it is for, each card showing what it costs and — for a machine — every recipe it runs, written as the materials in and the materials out. Click a recipe to hold that machine set to it. The bottom bar is yours to arrange: 1–9 select its nine slots, Pin drops a building into the first free one, and dragging a card onto a slot chooses which. Drag a slot onto another to swap them, or use the × to clear one. Your arrangement is remembered.

            Building

            With a belt or single-hex building selected, drag across the map to lay a whole run at once. Belts route around blocked cells when a bounded path is available. E selects erase, and dragging with it removes a run. R rotates the pending or selected building clockwise; Shift+R rotates it back. Q copies whatever is under the cursor, and Ctrl+Z takes back the last thing you built. Machines run one category of recipe each; smelters and kilns also need fuel, and burn whatever arrives. Once you have researched the longer step, R also turns a belt onto the six headings that run over the seam between two hexes; those belts reach twice as far, cost twice as much, and leave the hexes they cross free to build on. Splitters fan one lane into every free heading ahead and feed them in turn, mergers take from every lane behind and alternate between them, and a pair of underpasses carries a lane beneath the belts between them.

            Boxes and tiers

            Click a container to see everything in it. Each stored row has a Take, and each stack you are carrying has a Put, so stock moves both ways without a belt. Buildings with a higher tier show an Upgrade button that grows them in place, keeping their contents, their heading, and everything connected to them.

            Reading the ground

            Terrain is the material map, and hatched ground is ground you cannot stand on. Travelling toward the dashed survey frontier lifts the fog for good; the minimap and the gold marker on the edge of the view both point back to the landing hub.
