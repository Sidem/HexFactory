Moving and looking

Move with WASD. The player always faces the pointer. Space centres the camera back on the player, T pauses and resumes the factory, and dragging with the right or middle button pans while the wheel zooms.

Panels

I opens the cargo pack, O research, and P this panel. Esc closes whatever is open and returns to the inspect tool, which is the one panel that stays on the world.

Field actions

Hold F to keep gathering from the field you are standing on or beside, and X to deliver at the landing hub. The ring that closes around you is the wait until the next action.

Building

With a belt or single-hex building selected, drag across the map to lay a whole run at once — it routes itself, turning where the drag turns. E selects erase, and dragging with it removes a run. R rotates the pending building, or the one under the cursor when no build tool is held. Q copies whatever is under the cursor, and Ctrl+Z takes back the last thing you built. Machines run one category of recipe each; smelters and kilns also need fuel, and burn whatever arrives.

Reading the ground

Terrain is the material map, and hatched ground is ground you cannot stand on. Travelling toward the dashed survey frontier lifts the fog for good; the minimap and the gold marker on the edge of the view both point back to the landing hub.
