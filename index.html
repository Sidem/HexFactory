Controls & camera

Move with WASD and hold F to keep gathering. Drag with the right or middle button to pan, and use the wheel to zoom.

With a belt or single-hex building selected, drag across the map to lay a whole run at once — it routes itself, turning where the drag turns. E selects erase, and dragging with it removes a run. R rotates the pending building, or the one under the cursor when no build tool is held. Q copies whatever is under the cursor, and Ctrl+Z takes back the last thing you built.

Your pack holds a fixed number of stacks, so gathering, and recovering a building with something inside it, both stop when there is no slot free. Build a container, select it, and use the inspector to take stock back out a stack at a time.

The hatched veil past the dashed survey frontier is world the simulation has not generated yet. Travelling toward it surveys new sectors and lifts the fog for good.
